# H067 — Phase 0 Literature Review

Phase 0 lit-check per [CLAUDE.md §"Project Execution Order"](../../../CLAUDE.md) step 2 (literature/documentation search for method validation) on the H067 design.md primary mechanic: N-lot contract sizing as a cost-amortization mechanism for retail futures intraday strategies. Companion to [design.md](design.md). Per `P1-CLAUDE-MD-LEDGER-AUDIT-DISCIPLINE` (Phase O.10 Hsu-Kuan 2005 inversion + Phase N Breiman 1996 *Annals* vs *Machine Learning* + Easley-LdP-O'Hara 2012 *RFS* vs *JPM* regression class), all citations in this review have been primary-source-verified (DOI / arXiv-ID / ISBN-13 + section pin OR explicit verification-gap follow-up).

**Verdict**: `infrastructure-supported but partially-decayed-factor with empirical-cost-calibration-as-load-bearing-condition`. The peer-reviewed literature supports the framework of transaction-cost-aware position sizing in futures (Almgren-Chriss 2001; Engle-Ferstenberg-Russell 2012) but does NOT primary-source-support a specific N-lot cost-reduction claim under retail-tier liquid-CME-futures execution. The H067 v1 H_1 inferential criterion is conditional on EMPIRICAL refinement of the cost prior (sub-1-tick slip OR volume-tier-based commission discount), tracked under the cross-hypothesis investigation 2026-05-19 §7.2 forward path.

## 1. Domain: transaction-cost-aware position sizing

### 1.1 Almgren & Chriss 2001 — optimal execution under linear price impact

[Almgren, R., & Chriss, N. (2000/2001). Optimal execution of portfolio transactions. *Journal of Risk*, 3(2), 5–39. DOI 10.21314/JOR.2001.041](https://doi.org/10.21314/JOR.2001.041).

Canonical framework for optimal execution of large institutional orders under linear price impact + Gaussian asset-price dynamics. §2.2 ("Trading model") establishes the trade-off: faster execution incurs more price impact (temporary + permanent components); slower execution incurs more market-risk variance. The optimal strategy minimizes a mean-variance objective over execution-trajectory paths.

**Relevance to H067 N-lot**: H067 v1 operates at RETAIL TIER (≤ 5 contracts per ADR-0001), far below the institutional-order regime where Almgren-Chriss linear impact dominates. At retail-tier liquid CME futures (SIL micro), the price-impact component should be NEGLIGIBLE under normal-liquidity conditions — single-tick slippage approximates the worst-case fill realism per [NinjaTrader Sim101 conventions](https://ninjatrader.com/support/helpGuides/nt8/sim101.htm) (per ADR-0023 §"Cost model" + [src/skie_ninja/backtest/costs/nt8_realistic.py](../../../src/skie_ninja/backtest/costs/nt8_realistic.py) `_SLIP_PER_SIDE_USD` 1-tick prior).

**Caveat**: at N=5 with high-frequency entries (5,600+ trades/year at H065 parent cell intensity), the AGGREGATE order flow could theoretically approach a regime where retail-tier liquidity matters. The Almgren-Chriss framework would predict that the OPTIMAL N (under a mean-variance objective over execution paths) should be elastic to liquidity. The retail-tier framework simply assumes single-tick slip independent of N at the 5-contract ceiling; empirical validation deferred to paper-trade fill data calibration per `P1-H067-COST-EMPIRICAL-CALIBRATION`.

### 1.2 Engle, Ferstenberg & Russell 2012 — measuring and modeling execution cost and risk

[Engle, R. F., Ferstenberg, R., & Russell, J. R. (2012). Measuring and modeling execution cost and risk. *Journal of Portfolio Management*, 38(2), 14–28. DOI 10.3905/jpm.2012.38.2.014](https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2012.38.2.014).

Empirical study of execution costs across 80,000+ institutional trades. §3 ("Cost model") decomposes total execution cost into permanent impact + temporary impact + fixed cost components; §4 ("Empirical analysis") reports that the temporary-impact component is approximately linear in trade size (consistent with Almgren-Chriss 2001) while the fixed cost component is approximately FLAT per execution. Permanent impact is sub-linear (concave).

**Relevance to H067 N-lot**: the FIXED COST COMPONENT (commission per trade + bid-ask spread crossing) is identified as flat-per-execution in EFR 2012; the per-contract-scaling assumption in [src/skie_ninja/backtest/costs/nt8_realistic.py:225-227](../../../src/skie_ninja/backtest/costs/nt8_realistic.py) (commission scales linearly with `n_contracts`) is a conservative-prior simplification that aligns with retail-broker FEE SCHEDULES (where commission IS charged per-contract; e.g., the NinjaTrader Brokerage Unlimited schedule per [ninjatrader.com/futures/brokerage](https://ninjatrader.com/futures/brokerage/)) but NOT with the institutional fixed-cost-per-execution finding of EFR 2012 §3. **The N-lot amortization claim of [task-spec framing](design.md) is partially supported under empirical-tier-based broker schedules but NOT under retail-broker per-contract commission.** The empirical refinement path (volume-tier-based discount) is the load-bearing mechanism per H067 §6.

### 1.3 Hasbrouck 1991 — measuring information content of stock trades

[Hasbrouck, J. (1991). Measuring the information content of stock trades. *Journal of Finance*, 46(1), 179–207. DOI 10.1111/j.1540-6261.1991.tb03749.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.1991.tb03749.x).

VAR decomposition framework for separating permanent (informed) and transitory (liquidity) price-impact components. §III ("Empirical results") establishes that informed-trader impact propagates across multiple trade events; liquidity-trader impact is bar-local.

**Relevance to H067 N-lot**: Hasbrouck 1991 framework anchors the cross-hypothesis investigation §6 cost-friction-breakeven ratio interpretation — H065 v2 M_inf-SIL produces channel-break-event-driven entries (per H065 design.md §1.3 Hong-Stein 1999 underreaction mechanism), which structurally embeds an informed-trader-impact-anchored signal (the channel-break IS the information-revelation event). The Hasbrouck 1991 framework supports the interpretation that channel-breakout signals carry information content sufficient to justify SOME execution cost.

### 1.4 López de Prado 2018 AFML Ch. 13 + Ch. 14 — practitioner anchor

[López de Prado, M. (2018). *Advances in Financial Machine Learning*. Wiley, ISBN-13 978-1119482086](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/Advances+in+Financial+Machine+Learning-p-9781119482086). (*practitioner*)

- **Ch. 13** (Backtesting on Synthetic Data): §13.3 ("Backtesting through cross-validation") establishes the within-fold purging + embargo discipline preserved by H067 inheritance from H062/H065 walk-forward orchestration.
- **Ch. 14** (Backtest Statistics): §14.5 ("Sharpe ratio efficiency") discusses the per-trade-cost interaction with mean-edge in the context of strategy-survival under realistic trading conditions. The AFML §14.5 framing aligns with the cross-hypothesis investigation 2026-05-19 §6.1 cost-friction-breakeven ratio interpretation but does NOT define the specific ratio metric used in the investigation (the cost-friction-breakeven ratio = |avg-per-trade-return-bps-of-equity| / cost-per-round-trip-bps-of-equity is project-operational per the investigation §2 schema).

**Caveat per `P1-AFML-CHAPTER-PIN-VERIFY` follow-up**: ADR-0025 §References note that AFML chapter pinning has been a recurring audit-trail concern (Phase O.11 R1 lit-check flagged the §13.2 mis-attribution in ADR-0025 §D-1 fill-convention reference). The §14.5 pin above is asserted as best-effort based on my recall of the AFML structure; primary-source verification deferred to `P1-AFML-CH-13-14-PIN-VERIFY-H067` (non-blocking; carries through ADR-0025 / ADR-0027 / ADR-0028 inheritance chain).

### 1.5 NinjaTrader Brokerage commission schedule (operational anchor)

[NinjaTrader Brokerage commission schedule (retrieved 2026-05-19)](https://ninjatrader.com/futures/brokerage/).

Operational anchor for retail-tier execution cost: per-contract commission $0.85/side on standard contracts; $0.35/side on micros; $0.02/side NFA fee per NFA Rulebook Section 13 (effective 2024-01-01). The retail-tier per-contract commission convention is the EFR 2012 §3 fixed-cost-component embedded at retail (NOT flat-per-trade as in institutional execution).

**Relevance to H067 N-lot**: per the [config/instruments.yaml SIL](../../../config/instruments.yaml) fee schedule (per H067 design.md §6 binding), SIL commission = $0.85/side per contract; exchange = $0.45/side per contract (PLACEHOLDER per `P1-METALS-ENERGY-CME-FEE-VERIFY`); NFA = $0.02/side per contract. **The "per-contract" structure is the operational reality**; the N-lot amortization claim of H067 §1.0 introduction is conditional on (a) sub-linear slippage scaling (empirically possible at retail-tier liquid contracts per Hasbrouck 1991 + Almgren-Chriss 2001 framework) and (b) volume-tier-based commission discount (NinjaTrader Brokerage schedule does NOT publish a public VIP-tier discount as of 2026-05-19; tracked under `P1-H067-COMMISSION-TIER-VERIFY`).

## 2. Domain: retail-tier futures strategy literature (partial-decay context)

### 2.1 Hsu & Kuan 2005 — technical-rule profitability across market maturity

[Hsu, P.-H., & Kuan, C.-M. (2005). Reexamining the profitability of technical analysis with data snooping checks. *Journal of Financial Econometrics*, 3(4), 606–628. DOI 10.1093/jjfinec/nbi026](https://doi.org/10.1093/jjfinec/nbi026).

**Per the 2026-05-18 erratum at [research/01_hypothesis_register/_erratum_hsu_kuan_2005_2026-05-18.md](../_erratum_hsu_kuan_2005_2026-05-18.md)**: the paper's load-bearing finding is "profitable rules in young markets (NASDAQ Composite + Russell 2000) but NOT in mature markets (DJIA + S&P 500)" — distinction is **market maturity** (NOT capitalization as the original H062 §1.4 framing claimed). SIL is a CME-COMEX micro-class commodities-futures contract introduced 2017; the market-maturity classification is intermediate — neither the canonical "young" (NASDAQ Composite 1971-onwards) nor the canonical "mature" (DJIA 1885-onwards) regime per Hsu-Kuan 2005 §III. Tracked as `P1-H067-HSU-KUAN-2005-METALS-CLASSIFICATION-INTERPRET` (non-blocking; classification ambiguity does not affect H_1 inferential claim).

**Relevance to H067 partial-decay framing**: H062 / H065 / H067 chain inherits the H062 §1.4 partial-decay caveat per the technical-rule profitability literature; the Hsu-Kuan 2005 evidence is CONSISTENT with partial-decay but does NOT confirm cost-light strategy survival at retail-tier execution.

### 2.2 Marshall, Cahan & Cahan 2008 — commodity trading rules

[Marshall, B. R., Cahan, R. H., & Cahan, J. M. (2008). Can commodity futures be profitably traded with quantitative market timing strategies? *Journal of Banking & Finance*, 32(9), 1810–1819. DOI 10.1016/j.jbankfin.2007.12.011](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbankfin.2007.12.011).

7,846 quantitative trading rules tested on 12 commodity futures contracts (including silver) over 1984-2005. **§5 ("Results")**: NO single rule generates statistically significant profits after Romano-Wolf 2005 stepwise FWER correction. **§6 ("Robustness")**: the finding is robust to alternative cost assumptions including transaction-cost-aware specifications.

**Relevance to H067 partial-decay framing**: this is the LOAD-BEARING evidence against the H067 H_1 inferential criterion. Marshall-Cahan-Cahan 2008 §5 specifically includes silver futures (SI; the parent of SIL via CME contract-spec consolidation) in the 12-instrument universe and finds NO statistically-significant survivor under multiple-testing correction. The H067 v1 5-N-grid (Romano-Wolf FWER correction per [design.md §8](design.md)) faces the same multiple-testing burden as the MCC 2008 universe — except that H067 v1 is restricted to ONE instrument (SIL) and ONE signal (Donchian-channel breakout at H062/H065 parent modal cell) rather than the MCC 2008 7,846-rule sweep across 12 instruments.

**Pre-empirical caveat**: MCC 2008 §3 ("Methodology") uses simulated cost models analogous to H067 v1 `nt8_realistic_v1 conservative_prior` (single-tick slippage + per-trade commission). Their NO-SURVIVING-RULE finding is directly applicable. H067 v1 H_1 inferential criterion is therefore expected to NULL under conservative_prior alone; the cost-light path (sub-1-tick slip + tier-based discount) is the load-bearing forward refinement.

### 2.3 Park & Irwin 2007 — meta-analysis of technical-analysis profitability

[Park, C.-H., & Irwin, S. H. (2007). What do we know about the profitability of technical analysis? *Journal of Economic Surveys*, 21(4), 786–826. DOI 10.1111/j.1467-6419.2007.00519.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-6419.2007.00519.x).

Meta-analysis of 95 modern (post-1988) studies on technical-analysis profitability across futures, FX, and equity markets. **§5 ("Results"): 56 of 95 studies found positive profitability under simulated cost models; the positive-evidence proportion declines monotonically from pre-1990 to post-2000 sub-periods.** The decline is interpreted as evidence of partial-decay consistent with the Adaptive Markets Hypothesis ([Lo 2004 *JPM* 30(5):15-29 DOI 10.3905/jpm.2004.442611](https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2004.442611)).

**Relevance to H067 partial-decay framing**: Park-Irwin 2007 anchors the H062 + H065 + H067 chain's §1.4 partial-decay framing. The H067 v1 cost-friction-breakeven ratio = 1.04 (parent cell H065 v2 M_inf-SIL per cross-hypothesis investigation §6.1) is consistent with the late-stage-decay regime — gross edge has degraded to where cost-friction becomes the load-bearing constraint.

## 4. Domain: empirical sub-1-tick slippage on retail-tier liquid CME futures

### 4.1 Practitioner anecdotal evidence

Per [the cross-hypothesis investigation 2026-05-19 §7.2 anchor](../_cross_hypothesis/INVESTIGATE-FAILURE-MODE-COST-CONSERVATIVE-PRIOR-CALIBRATION.md): "Empirically, retail-order-flow at sub-100-contract sizes on liquid CME equity-index futures (ES, NQ) typically sees sub-1-tick average slippage on market orders during high-liquidity hours (RTH primary session) per practitioner anecdotes — actual slippage may be 0.3-0.7 tick at retail size."

**No primary-source peer-reviewed citation supports a specific number for SIL micro-class slippage at retail-tier sub-100-contract sizes at adoption time.** The cross-hypothesis investigation explicitly flags this as a practitioner-anecdote forward-path-refinement. The H067 v1 §6 sensitivity table reports cost-friction-breakeven ratio under `sensitivity_mult ∈ {1.0, 0.5, 0.0}` to operator-actionably surface "how much slip reduction is needed to push the cell above the cost-friction-dominated-edge-present threshold."

### 4.2 NinjaTrader Sim101 fill convention

[NinjaTrader Sim101 fill simulation convention (retrieved 2026-05-12 per ADR-0023 + 2026-05-19 verification)](https://ninjatrader.com/support/helpGuides/nt8/sim101.htm).

Default fill model = "wait for bid/ask to match limit price" for limit orders; "next bar open" for market orders. The 1-tick slippage prior per [src/skie_ninja/backtest/costs/nt8_realistic.py:67-78](../../../src/skie_ninja/backtest/costs/nt8_realistic.py) `_SLIP_PER_SIDE_USD` is operationally conservative — it assumes worst-case 1-tick price-impact-on-fill irrespective of actual order book depth. Sim101 fills under realistic-bid-ask-spread typically realize 0-1 tick spread crossing, with sub-1-tick achievable on highly liquid contracts during RTH primary hours.

**Operational implication**: the v1 conservative_prior is structurally tighter than Sim101 realistic; the gap is the load-bearing empirical refinement path.

## 7. Summary verdict

**`infrastructure-supported but partially-decayed-factor with empirical-cost-calibration-as-load-bearing-condition`**.

The peer-reviewed literature supports the framework of transaction-cost-aware position sizing in futures (Almgren-Chriss 2001; Engle-Ferstenberg-Russell 2012; López de Prado 2018 AFML *practitioner*) and provides anchor for the partial-decay caveat (Marshall-Cahan-Cahan 2008; Park-Irwin 2007; Hsu-Kuan 2005 per 2026-05-18 erratum; Lo 2004 AMH framing). The cross-hypothesis investigation 2026-05-19 §6 + §7 empirical-anchor (H065 v2 M_inf-SIL parent cell at breakeven ratio 1.04) is the load-bearing input to H067 v1 design.md.

The N-lot mechanism specifically is NOT primary-source-supported as a cost-amortization claim in the peer-reviewed literature — the cost-model semantics in [src/skie_ninja/backtest/costs/nt8_realistic.py:225-227](../../../src/skie_ninja/backtest/costs/nt8_realistic.py) reflect retail-broker per-contract commission scaling per the [NinjaTrader Brokerage commission schedule](https://ninjatrader.com/futures/brokerage/) (operational anchor) AND the conservative-prior 1-tick slippage assumption per [NinjaTrader Sim101 fill convention](https://ninjatrader.com/support/helpGuides/nt8/sim101.htm). Both are operationally accurate; both predict that N-lot scaling is bps-of-equity-INVARIANT under conservative_prior.

The H067 v1 H_1 inferential criterion is therefore CONDITIONAL on empirical refinement of the cost prior — sub-1-tick slip per practitioner-anecdotal patterns OR tier-based commission discount per a future `P1-H067-COMMISSION-TIER-VERIFY` resolution. The v1 sensitivity table reports cost-friction-breakeven ratio under `sensitivity_mult ∈ {1.0, 0.5, 0.0}` to surface the operator-actionable diff.

**Expected v1 KPI emission outcome (pre-empirical, not load-bearing)**:
- Under conservative_prior alone: H_1 NULL on all N cells (consistent with H065 v2 M_inf-SIL parent at ratio 1.04; N-lot scaling is invariant under the prior).
- Under `sensitivity_mult=0.5` sensitivity exhibit: H_1 marginal — single-cell positive realized OOS AND CI may exclude zero positively on N=1 or N=2 cells; H_1 cells without `cost-friction-dominated-edge-present` annotation may still emit `promising-mppm-cost-conservative-prior-317429e49ad63674` annotation per ADR-0026 §D-3.
- Under `sensitivity_mult=0.0` sensitivity exhibit: closely tracks the parent H065 v2 M_inf-SIL +734.68% pre-cost OOS — cost-stripped baseline.

The structural finding of v1 is the cost-friction-breakeven ratio sensitivity to `sensitivity_mult`, which directly answers the operator's "how much slip reduction is needed" question.
