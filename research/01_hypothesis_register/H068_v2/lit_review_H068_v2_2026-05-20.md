**Verdict**: `infrastructure-supported + multi-instrument-basket-aggregation-as-inferential-power-lift-mechanism`. The peer-reviewed literature on multi-asset signal aggregation (Grinold 1989 Fundamental Law of Active Management; Moskowitz-Ooi-Pedersen 2012 TSMOM cross-asset breadth) supports the framework of basket-aggregation-as-statistical-power-lift. The H068 v1 → H068 v2 universe expansion is a canonical instance of the Grinold 1989 √breadth multiplier mechanic applied at the statistical-power layer (narrower CI under same per-trade mean-edge) rather than the alpha-aggregation layer (basket return higher than per-symbol returns). ## 1. Domain: multi-asset signal aggregation + Fundamental Law of Active Management

### 1.1 Grinold 1989 — Fundamental Law of Active Management

[Grinold, R. C. (1989). The fundamental law of active management. *Journal of Portfolio Management*, 15(3), 30-37. DOI 10.3905/jpm.1989.409211](https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.1989.409211).

Canonical relationship `IR ≈ IC × √breadth` (Information Ratio ≈ Information Coefficient × square-root of number of independent signal bets). §III ("Adding strategies to a portfolio") establishes that the IR scales by √breadth under uncorrelated-signal assumption; correlated signals scale by a smaller factor (the effective-breadth correction per the [Clarke-de Silva-Thorley 2002 *FAJ*](https://doi.org/10.2469/faj.v58.n5.2467) Transfer-Coefficient extension).

**Relevance to H068 v2**: The H068 v2 universe expansion from ES standalone (breadth=1) to {ES, MGC, SIL} 3-symbol basket (effective breadth ≈ 2.4-2.6 under typical ES-MGC + ES-SIL + MGC-SIL correlation magnitudes per the Phase O.0 metals/energy substrate Pearson-correlation analysis) provides a **√breadth multiplier ≈ √2.5 ≈ 1.58** at the basket-aggregated IR layer. **The H068 v2 KPI report card SHALL surface this multiplier explicitly** by reporting the basket-aggregated MPPM(ρ=1) CI width vs the H068 v1 ES-only CI width (expected ratio ~1/1.58 ≈ 0.63 vs the naive √(n_v2/n_v1) ≈ 1/8.2 = 0.12 expectation; the n_trades-weighted average per the basket-aggregation pattern dominates the breadth multiplier at this trade-count asymmetry).

**Caveat per verification gap**: pm-research.com (JPM publisher) is auth-walled per recurring `P1-H060-CITE-DOI-VERIFY-JPM-PMRESEARCH` precedent at Phase O.0 + the H068 v1 lit-review §1.2 verification gap. Grinold 1989 §III specific equation pin recorded as best-effort under `P1-H068-V2-GRINOLD-1989-DOI-VERIFY` (non-blocking; mirrors H068 v1 + H067 + H060 precedent).

### 1.2 DeMiguel-Garlappi-Uppal 2009 — equal-weighted 1/N portfolio robustness

[DeMiguel, V., Garlappi, L., & Uppal, R. (2009). Optimal versus naive diversification: How inefficient is the 1/N portfolio strategy? *Review of Financial Studies*, 22(5), 1915-1953. DOI 10.1093/rfs/hhm075](https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhm075).

Empirical study of 14 portfolio allocation strategies across 7 different datasets. **§3 ("Empirical evaluation")**: under small-N estimation noise, equal-weighted 1/N portfolio matches or outperforms sophisticated MVO strategies (mean-variance, minimum-variance, Bayes-Stein shrinkage, factor-model). The breakeven sample-size for MVO to outperform 1/N is impractically large (≥ 6,000 monthly observations for a 25-asset universe).

**Relevance to H068 v2**: The H068 v2 basket-aggregation uses **equal-weighted 1/N (per-symbol equal-weight)** per [design.md §1](design.md). The DGU 2009 §3 finding supports the H068 v2 default 1/N basket weighting under the H068 v1 small-sample regime (n_trades=108 per cell); MVO/shrinkage weights would NOT outperform 1/N at this sample size. The v2 default equal-weighting is therefore literature-anchored, not arbitrary.

**Operational implication for H068 v2**: per-symbol independent sizing at v2 with per-symbol equal-weighted basket aggregation is the [DGU 2009 §3](https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhm075) literature-anchored default. Correlation-aware allocation (mean-variance or shrinkage) deferred to MPV2 cycle per `P1-MPV2-PER-SESSION-RETURNS-INTEGRATION` (CLAUDE.md Phase O.4 ledger entry).

### 1.3 Clarke-de Silva-Thorley 2002 — Transfer Coefficient extension

[Clarke, R., de Silva, H., & Thorley, S. (2002). Portfolio constraints and the fundamental law of active management. *Financial Analysts Journal*, 58(5), 48-66. DOI 10.2469/faj.v58.n5.2467](https://doi.org/10.2469/faj.v58.n5.2467).

Extends Grinold 1989 with a Transfer Coefficient (TC) correction: `IR = IC × √breadth × TC` where TC measures the implementation efficiency (how well the actual portfolio captures the signal). §IV ("Constrained portfolios") establishes that long-only + position-cap + sector-constraint restrictions reduce TC to 0.3-0.6 in equity-portfolio settings.

**Relevance to H068 v2**: H068 v2 operates at retail-tier with K-4 capacity caps + 1-lot-per-cell sizing. The Clarke-de Silva-Thorley 2002 §IV TC framework would suggest the H068 v2 effective IR is some fraction TC × Grinold IR; the v2 KPI report card §"Performance KPIs" SHALL report per-symbol n_trades + per-symbol position-cap utilization as descriptive surfaces for the operator-discretionary TC interpretation. TC quantification per cell is non-blocking at v2.

### 1.4 Moskowitz-Ooi-Pedersen 2012 — cross-asset breadth in TSMOM

[Moskowitz, T. J., Ooi, Y. H., & Pedersen, L. H. (2012). Time series momentum. *Journal of Financial Economics*, 104(2), 228-250. DOI 10.1016/j.jfineco.2011.11.003](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2011.11.003).

Canonical empirical study of time-series momentum across 58 instruments (24 commodity futures + 12 equity-index futures + 9 bond futures + 13 currency forwards) over 1985-2009. §4 ("Time series momentum across asset classes") demonstrates that basket aggregation across multiple instruments improves the Sharpe ratio of the TSMOM signal via the Grinold 1989 √breadth multiplier — the cross-asset 58-instrument basket achieves Sharpe ~1.0 vs typical per-instrument Sharpe ~0.3-0.5.

**Relevance to H068 v2**: MOP 2012 §4 is the canonical empirical demonstration of multi-instrument basket aggregation lifting IR via the Grinold 1989 mechanic. The H068 v2 3-symbol {ES, MGC, SIL} basket is a smaller version of the same construct on the Donchian channel-breakout signal class rather than the TSMOM 12-month signal. The structural mechanic (basket aggregation → IR lift via √breadth) is identical.

**Caveat**: MOP 2012 §6 ("Performance and theoretical insights") establishes that the per-instrument TSMOM Sharpe is ~0.3-0.5 in MOP's sample; subsequent literature (Huang-Li-Wang-Zhou 2020 *JFE* 137(3):695-712 DOI 10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.04.003; Baltas-Kosowski 2013 SSRN 1968996) documents post-publication decay in the cross-asset TSMOM. The H068 v2 basket aggregation does NOT inherit the TSMOM signal class; H068 v2 inherits the Donchian channel-breakout signal class. The Grinold 1989 multiplier mechanic applies independent of signal-class — basket aggregation lifts IR via √breadth regardless of the underlying signal class — but the per-instrument signal-class mean-edge is a separate consideration (the H068 v1 §1.4 partial-decay framing is preserved at v2).

### 1.5 Ledoit-Wolf 2003 — covariance shrinkage for portfolio aggregation

[Ledoit, O., & Wolf, M. (2003). Improved estimation of the covariance matrix of stock returns with an application to portfolio selection. *Journal of Empirical Finance*, 10(5), 603-621. DOI 10.1016/S0927-5398(03)00007-0](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0927-5398(03)00007-0).

Shrinkage estimator for the covariance matrix between Sharpe-style asset-return distributions, anchored on the constant-correlation Frobenius-norm-minimizing target. §3 ("Empirical evaluation") demonstrates that the shrinkage estimator outperforms the sample covariance matrix at small-N estimation regimes (≤ 60 monthly observations × ≤ 20 assets).

**Relevance to H068 v2**: H068 v2 uses equal-weighted 1/N basket aggregation per §1.2 above; covariance-aware aggregation deferred to MPV2 per `P1-MPV2-PER-SESSION-RETURNS-INTEGRATION`. When MPV2 lands, the Ledoit-Wolf 2003 shrinkage is the literature-anchored covariance-aware allocation primitive; v2 1/N equal-weighting is the DGU 2009 §3 robustness anchor at the H068 v2 small-N (3 symbols) regime. The LW 2003 shrinkage would be more relevant at a larger universe (≥ 10 symbols) where the constant-correlation target adds material estimation efficiency.

## 2. Domain: per-symbol vs basket-aggregated inferential testing

### 2.1 Romano-Wolf 2005 — stepwise FWER multiple testing

[Romano, J. P., & Wolf, M. (2005). Stepwise multiple testing as formalized data snooping. *Econometrica*, 73(4), 1237-1282. DOI 10.1111/j.1468-0262.2005.00615.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1468-0262.2005.00615.x).

Stepwise familywise-error-rate (FWER) multiple-testing correction generalizing the Bonferroni-Holm step-down to dependent-test-statistic settings via bootstrap-based critical-value tables. §2 ("Stepwise testing") establishes the canonical bootstrap-resampling algorithm; §4 ("Empirical application") demonstrates the method on trading-rule families.

**Relevance to H068 v2**: H068 v2 per [design.md §8](design.md) applies Romano-Wolf 2005 stepwise FWER at the 3-slip-cell family level (3 slip values; basket-aggregated H_1 evaluation per slip cell). The family size at the basket level remains 3 per H068 v1 §1 convention; per-symbol cells emit as descriptive supplementary cells without independent family-wise correction at v2 (operator-discretionary per-symbol promotion per ADR-0013 §1 + ADR-0027 §D-4 single-axis discipline). RW 2005 is the canonical reference for the FWER discipline.

### 2.2 Bailey-López de Prado 2014 — Deflated Sharpe Ratio

[Bailey, D. H., & López de Prado, M. (2014). The deflated Sharpe ratio: Correcting for selection bias, backtest overfitting, and non-normality. *Journal of Portfolio Management*, 40(5), 94-107. DOI 10.3905/jpm.2014.40.5.094](https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2014.40.5.094).

Deflated Sharpe Ratio (DSR) corrects for backtest-overfitting selection bias. §3 ("Deflated Sharpe Ratio") establishes the analytical correction for the probability of observing the maximum Sharpe ratio across N independent trials under the null hypothesis of zero true-edge.

**Relevance to H068 v2**: H068 v2 per [design.md §8](design.md) applies DSR deflation since realized cell count at the basket level (3 slip × 6 Kelly = 18 cells) ≥ project's `dsr_activation_size = 10` per [config/gate.yaml](../../../config/gate.yaml). BLdP 2014 §3 DSR is the canonical deflation primitive. Per CLAUDE.md ADR-0027 §D-2 reaffirmation, DSR is mandatory at v2 emission.

**Caveat per verification gap**: pm-research.com (JPM publisher) auth-walled per `P1-H060-CITE-DOI-VERIFY-JPM-PMRESEARCH`. BLdP 2014 §3 specific equation pins recorded as best-effort under `P1-H068-V2-BLDP-2014-DOI-VERIFY` (non-blocking; mirrors H068 v1 + H067 + H060 precedent).
