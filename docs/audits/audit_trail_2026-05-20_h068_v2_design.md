| **F-Q-1** (critical): cell-budget arithmetic — 3 symbols × 3 slip × 6 Kelly × 2 cost-arm = 108 per-symbol cells; basket-aggregated cells = 3 × 6 × 2 = 36. Total emitted cells in design.md §5 declared as 108 (per-symbol) NOT 144 (per-symbol + basket-aggregated). | critical | **REMEDIATED inline at design.md §5 cell-budget table + config YAML `cell_budget.total_emitted_cells: 144`**. The basket-aggregated cells are STATISTICAL re-aggregations of the 108 per-symbol cell results (basket-aggregated per-session log-return is the equal-weighted sum across 3 symbols × per-session log-returns from each per-symbol cell); they are emitted as SEPARATE 36 cells in the KPI report card §"End-of-simulation results summary" but do NOT consume additional walk-forward simulation compute. The 108-cell figure is the WALK-FORWARD-COMPUTE cell budget; the 144-cell figure is the EMITTED-OUTPUT cell budget per ADR-0014 §3.2 13-table re-presentation. **Fix verified**: design.md §5 cell-budget table + config YAML are consistent at 108 walk-forward cells + 36 basket-aggregated cells + 144 total emitted cells. |
| **F-Q-2** (major): Grinold 1989 √breadth multiplier expected basket-aggregated CI width estimation in design.md §9 + lit_review §1.1: claimed 1/√(effective_breadth ≈ 2.5) ≈ 0.63 with naive √(n_v2/n_v1) ≈ 0.12 comparison. The actual basket-aggregated MPPM(ρ=1) CI width scales by what factor? | major | **REMEDIATED inline at design.md §9 + lit_review §1.1**. The basket-aggregated per-session log-return is the equal-weighted (1/3) sum across symbols; per-symbol log-returns have non-zero correlation (rough estimate per ADR-0023 + H062 v2 cross-asset substrate: ES-MGC ~0.1, ES-SIL ~0.05, MGC-SIL ~0.7 — metals correlated; equity-index uncorrelated to metals at session-cadence). Variance of basket per-session log-return = (1/9) × [Var(ES) + Var(MGC) + Var(SIL) + 2×Cov(ES,MGC) + 2×Cov(ES,SIL) + 2×Cov(MGC,SIL)]. Effective breadth per [Clarke-de Silva-Thorley 2002 §IV TC-extension](https://doi.org/10.2469/faj.v58.n5.2467) ≈ 2.5 under MGC-SIL correlation ≈ 0.7. CI width scales NOT by 1/√(n_v2/n_v1) (≈ 1/8.2 = 0.12 naive) NOR purely by 1/√(effective_breadth) (≈ 0.63 Grinold) — but by the n-weighted-AVERAGE of per-symbol CIs adjusted for correlation. **The actual basket-aggregated CI width is empirically determined at v2 emission**; the lit_review §1.1 + design.md §9 framing pre-empirical estimate is correctly bounded between the 0.12 (uncorrelated n-pool) and 0.63 (correlation-adjusted breadth) endpoints. The empirical CI width is the load-bearing v2 KPI emission output; pre-empirical estimate range is documented inline as "expected basket-aggregated CI between ~0.12 (under independence assumption) and ~0.63 (under Grinold √breadth with MGC-SIL correlation 0.7); v2 KPI emission resolves the empirical value." |

This is the standard cross-session-boundary handling for equity-index + metals basket per H062 + H055 v2 + H060 v2 precedent. |
